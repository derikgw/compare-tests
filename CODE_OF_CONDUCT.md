# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Focus feedback on ideas and implementation details, not people.
- Accept and act on good-faith feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Scope

This applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions,
and review interactions.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to project maintainers through a private channel when
possible. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

This document is a lightweight starter policy for an early-stage project and
may evolve over time.
